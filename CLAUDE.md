# Salon Sucasa — concept website (spec build #5)

Concept/spec website for **Salon Sucasa**, 15004 S.R. 1 (Leo Rd),
Leo-Cedarville, IN 46765 · (260) 376-1277. Upscale boutique salon in a
restored historic building, owned by Sarah Shultz (opened Dec 26, 2018).
Built by It's Already Done Web Co. from public information — **Sarah has not
seen or approved it**; footer carries the concept disclaimer. Portfolio role:
**elegant/editorial** style (contrast to warm/playful/heritage/dark builds).
salonsucasa.com is UNREGISTERED — pitch hook.

  services $50+; 24-hour cancellation policy (soften the all-caps original)

  NY Fashion Week, Modern Salon feature, industry educator, photography

  Elise Biddle (dimensional/lived-in color, Perfectress extensions),

- Story (KPC News Jan 2020): restored dated brick building on Leo's main
  street; brick uncovered from under plaster; original tin ceiling & floors;
  soot marks from a fire "over 75 years ago" kept on purpose; 200-person
  open house; name = "mi casa es su casa" / "my second home"
- WORDING RULES: say "carrying Aveda, EVO & Back of Bottle" (NOT "Aveda
  concept salon"); do NOT claim building age/prior use or Chicago-brick as
  fact; do NOT tie "Sucasa" to Latin heritage (unsupported); hours conflict —

## Design

Elegant editorial: ivory #faf8f4, ink #2b2622, warm taupe, brass accent
#b08d57. Fonts: Cormorant Garamond (display) + Jost (body, light). Hairline
rules, letterspaced uppercase labels, generous whitespace. 3 pages: index /
services / salon (story + team + visit). Images via Gemini
(tools/gen_image.mjs, key in .env, gitignored).
