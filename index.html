The Building

The Brick Remembers

When Sarah Shultz restored this old main-street building, she peeled the plaster back to hundred-year brick, kept the original tin ceiling — and left the soot marks from a fire more than seventy-five years ago exactly where they were. "It was an awesome characteristic."

Two hundred neighbors came to the open house. The name says the rest: mi casa es su casa — my house is your house.
